Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by one family. When a family member connects their Google account, Hermes reads and manages that account's email, calendar, files, documents and tasks only to carry out that family member's own requests.

The data is processed on the family's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Requests are sent to the AI model provider that runs the assistant, only as needed to answer them.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the sign-in consent screen.
